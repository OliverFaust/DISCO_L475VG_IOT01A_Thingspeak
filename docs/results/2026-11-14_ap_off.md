- **No upload lost or duplicated on ThingSpeak:** the 6 accepted uploads are the 6 entries in the
  feed, with the printed values; the feed has no entries during the outages. A sample taken while the
  network is down is not queued: the Uplink always sends the newest sample (KeepNewest).
- **The rejoin starts at once:** after the first failed upload the driver reports the station as
  disconnected (`IsConnected()`), so the Uplink leaves and rejoins without waiting for three failures.
- **Join attempts while the AP is off** fail alternately with `-3` (`ARM_DRIVER_ERROR_TIMEOUT`, after
  16 s) and `-1` (after 0.6..8 s) -- the same pattern as with a wrong password
  (`2026-11-12_uplink_recovery.md`). The console cannot tell the two apart; the bring-up scan can.
- **Recovery time** is at most one back-off interval after the AP returns: up to 60 s after a long
  outage, plus the join (about 5 s).
- **Sensors and Console unaffected:** 254 samples in 1265 s, none overwritten, no sensor error.
- **Heap and stacks unchanged:** at 60, 660 and 1260 s and over SWD after the run: FreeRTOS heap
  minimum ever free 640 of 1024 bytes (`xMinimumEverFreeBytesRemaining = 0x280`),
  `__sbrk_heap_end = 0`; stack peaks Sensors 189, Console 251, Uplink 225 words (as in the soak).

## Finding: which hotspots the ISM43362 can use

Before this run, two phone hotspots did not work (`2026-11-14_hotspot_scans_console.txt`, masked):

1. **5 GHz only** (2026-11-12): the PC saw the hotspot on channel 100 (5500 MHz); the module's scan did
   not list it, and joins failed (`-3`/`-1`, as above). The phone offered no 2.4 GHz setting. The
   ISM43362 is a 2.4 GHz module.
2. **2.4 GHz, channel 13** (2026-11-14): the PC saw the hotspot on channel 13 (2472 MHz) at full
   signal; the module's scan did not list it. After the hotspot was moved to **channel 1**, the scan
   listed it (-50 dBm) and the board joined at once. Every access point the module has reported so
   far was on channels 1..11; with its current settings it evidently does not use channels 12 and 13
   (legal in Europe, not in the US).

For labs: a 2.4 GHz WPA2 hotspot on a channel between 1 and 11. Check with the bring-up test
(`APP_DIAG_WIFI_BRINGUP`) that the network appears in the scan before looking for other causes.
